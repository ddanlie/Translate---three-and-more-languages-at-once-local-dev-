Here is how you can name your service

For default CRUD do not use services - use simple functions from 
src/services/utils/axios_api_utils.ts

Template:

service_<serviceGroup>_<specificBehavior>_<sync/async>_<additionalInfo>

Examples:

- service_catalog_only-available-items_async_behavior-is-not-written-here

- service_user_make-score-zero_sync_this-is-repeating-task-not-general-crud-operation





Here is how you can name your query/mutation (useQuery/useMutation)

use_qery_<full service name>

use_mutation_<full service name>
